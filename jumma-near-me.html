Why “Jumma near me” needs more than a map

Searching “mosque near me” on a map tells you where masjids are. It does not tell you what time their Friday jamat begins — and that is the part that actually matters when you are travelling and have a narrow window before the khutbah.

Two things make Jumma times hard to guess:

  • They move with the sun. Jumma replaces Dhuhr, so its time shifts gradually through the year.
  • They differ by masjid. One masjid may start at 12:45 PM, another at 1:30 PM, and busy masjids run two or three Jummah congregations.

Takbeer Time solves this with crowd-sourced updates: local worshippers and masjid timekeepers post the current Jumma time, so when you search “Jumma near me” you get the real time at a masjid you can actually reach.
