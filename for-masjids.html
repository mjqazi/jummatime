How it works

Get your masjid on the map in minutes

STEP 01

Install & sign in

Download the free Takbeer Time app and create a timekeeper account.

STEP 02

Find or add your masjid

Search for your masjid. If it isn't listed yet, add it with its location and name.

STEP 03

Submit your times

Enter Jumma and daily jamat times. Add a second or third Jummah if your masjid holds them.

STEP 04

Keep them current

Update times whenever they change. Followers stay notified, and travelers always see the right time.

Why publish your Jummah time?

  • Travelers find you. People searching “jumma near me” in your area see your masjid with the correct, current time instead of an out-of-date guess.
  • Fewer wrong-time arrivals. Accurate times mean fewer people turning up after the khutbah or at the wrong masjid.
  • You stay in control. As a verified timekeeper, you manage your masjid's times and can review community-suggested corrections.
  • It's sadaqah jariyah. Every person who prays Jumma on time because of your update is ongoing reward, in shaa Allah.

Quick answers

For masjids — FAQ

How do I publish my masjid's Jummah time?

Install the free Takbeer Time app, sign in, find or add your masjid, and submit its Jumma and daily jamat times. Once published, anyone searching for Jumma near them will see your masjid's correct time.

Can I add more than one Jummah congregation?

Yes. You can add multiple Jummah times — Jummah 1, Jummah 2, Jummah 3 — so worshippers can pick the congregation they can reach.

Is it free for masjids?

Yes. Publishing and updating your masjid's times is completely free, with no ads. It is offered as sadaqah fi sabilillah.
