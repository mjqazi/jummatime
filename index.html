                        Time keeper ratings

                        One masjid can have several time keepers.

                        JummaTime chooses the highest-rated time keeper by default, so the app starts with the timings most likely to be trusted.

                        Rating uses votes, fresh updates, verification, contribution history, and keeper trust.

                        Followers show how many people use that keeper's timings.

                        To use someone else's timings, open a masjid, scroll to Time keepers, and tap "Use this time keeper's timings."

                        Delete your account?

                        This is permanent. Here's exactly what happens:

                        • Your account row (email, name, password hash, Firebase ID) is removed.
                        • Your favourites, default masjid, preferred keepers, and reminder preferences are deleted.
                        • Suggestions you've sent to time-keepers are deleted.
                        • Submitted jamat timings stay in the directory but are re-attributed as "Deleted user," because other users are relying on them right now.
                        • Your push-notification subscriptions on this device are dropped.
                        • You'll be signed out and the page will reload.

                        If you want specific submissions removed too, email qazi.junaid@gmail.com.
