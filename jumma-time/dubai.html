Quick answer

Jumma in Dubai is not one fixed city-wide time. It is prayed after Dhuhr, and each masjid sets its own jamat time, often between 12:30 PM and 2:00 PM. To find the current Jumma time near you in Dubai, tap “Find masjids near me” or install the free Takbeer Time app.

Finding Jumma in Dubai

Dubai has masjids in nearly every neighbourhood, and Jumma times are closely tied to the official Dhuhr time, which shifts through the year.

Because Jumma times move with Dhuhr and differ from masjid to masjid, a generic prayer-time estimate is not enough. Takbeer Time gives you masjid-level Jumma and jamat times that the local community keeps current. If you spot a Jumma time on a masjid door in Dubai, you can post it to help the next traveler.

Traveler tip: In Dubai the Jumma khutbah usually begins very close to the official Dhuhr time — check the exact minute in the app.

Jumma in Dubai is prayed after midday on Friday, replacing Dhuhr. The exact jamat time is set by each masjid and shifts through the year with Dhuhr (Gulf Standard Time). Use the Find masjids near me button or the Takbeer Time app for the current time at a masjid near you.

Do masjids in Dubai hold more than one Jummah?
