# Offsite SEO Playbook

Use this checklist to build authority for Jumma Time without creating low-quality links.

## Priority audiences

- Masjid admins who publish Jummah and daily jamat times.
- Muslim student associations and workplace prayer coordinators.
- Muslim travel bloggers and city guides.
- Local Islamic centers, community directories, and halal/travel resource pages.

## Linkable assets

- Home: https://jummatime.com/
- Jummah near me: https://jummatime.com/jumma-near-me.html
- What time is Jummah: https://jummatime.com/what-time-is-jumma.html
- Traveling guide: https://jummatime.com/jumma-while-traveling.html
- For masjids: https://jummatime.com/for-masjids.html
- City pages: https://jummatime.com/jumma-time/
- Android app: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.takbeertime.android
- Web app: https://takbeertime.com/

## Outreach angles

- "Help travelers find your correct Friday prayer time."
- "Free, no ads: publish Jummah and jamat times for your community."
- "City-specific Jummah guides for visitors, students, and shift workers."
- "Reduce phone calls to the masjid by keeping public Jummah times current."

## Suggested anchor text

- Jummah time near me
- Jumma time in [city]
- Friday prayer times at nearby masjids
- Publish masjid Jummah times
- Takbeer Time masjid prayer times

Use natural anchors. Avoid repeating the same exact-match phrase on every placement.

## Acquisition checklist

- Ask verified masjids listed in Takbeer Time to link to their city page or the For Masjids page.
- Add Jumma Time to Muslim community directories where the listing can include a real description.
- Pitch Muslim travel resources with the traveling guide and major city pages.
- Ask student Islamic societies to link to the nearest city guide in their prayer resources.
- Add links from Takbeer Time properties back to Jumma Time where contextually useful.
- Share city pages in local WhatsApp/Telegram/community groups only when the page has useful local masjid data.
- Encourage Android users to review Takbeer Time after successfully finding Jummah.

## Quality rules

- Do not buy links or place links on unrelated directories.
- Do not mass-submit spun descriptions.
- Prefer links from pages about prayer times, masjids, Muslim travel, campus prayer, or local Muslim communities.
- Prioritize pages where users would genuinely benefit from the link.

## Measurement

- Track referral traffic in GA4 by source/medium.
- Track city-page landing traffic and app-click events.
- Record outreach targets, placement URLs, anchor text, date, and resulting traffic in a simple spreadsheet.
