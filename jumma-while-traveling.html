Why traveling makes Jumma hard — and how to solve it

At home you know your masjid and its Jumma time by heart. On the road, both unknowns hit at once: which masjid, and what time. Map apps answer the first but not the second, and Jumma times move with the season and differ between masjids — so even a masjid's old website can mislead you.

A crowd-sourced directory fixes this. In Takbeer Time, local worshippers and timekeepers keep each masjid's Jumma time current, so a traveler opening the app in an unfamiliar city sees the same accurate time a local would.

If you miss Jumma while traveling

It happens — delayed flights, traffic, meetings. If you miss Jumma, you pray Dhuhr instead. But before you leave that masjid, take ten seconds to post its Jumma time in the app. Your update can help the next traveler searching “jumma near me” arrive on time — a small act of sadaqah jariyah.
